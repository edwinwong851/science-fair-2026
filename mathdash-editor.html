Math Dash Level Editor

Choose a tool, then click or drag on the grid to build a level. The level extends as you build, up to 400 columns. Your levels are saved in this browser and will appear in Math Dash on this device.

Tools

Use the horizontal scrollbar or Left/Right arrow keys to move around. The editor expands as you build, up to 400 columns (about 16,000 game pixels). The green line marks the finish. Place blocks along the bottom to make a floor. Erase removes a tile.
